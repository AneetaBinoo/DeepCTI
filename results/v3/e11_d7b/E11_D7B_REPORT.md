## Caveats

1. Post hoc: DCv21b, its validator check and this comparison were specified after the H15 result and the FIXED finding were known (D23). The H15 margin and test are reused, but the comparison is exploratory and carries no confirmatory weight; no multiplicity correction is applied.
2. Same sample: DCv21b was run on exactly the H15 triples (and evaluated with the same judges), so the result is not an independent replication; it isolates the effect of the synthesis-prompt change on these episodes.
3. DCv21b's strict validator rejects notes asserting another status, so part of any consistency gain could be by construction (such notes are repaired or replaced by the deterministic fallback). In these records the strict check fired on attempt 1 for only 1 of 504 notes, so the change is almost entirely due to the neutral prompt wording; judge-based consistency is reported regardless.
4. Generation and judging are at temperature 0; the D7 world physics and episodes are those sealed under prereg-v3. DC/DCv21 numbers are numerically identical to E11-D7 (reproduction check above).
