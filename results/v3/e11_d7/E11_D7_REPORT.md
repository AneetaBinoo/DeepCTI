Generated by `scripts/e11/d7/run_all_d7.sh` (all LLM calls cached in `results/v3/e11_d7/cache/`). Pre-registration: `prereg/PREREGISTRATION_V3.md` §3 (H15, secondary). ## Result

**H15 does not hold** (non-inferiority, margin −0.02 on the lower 95% CI bound). Claim faithfulness, pooled over 7 generators × 3 arms (504 paired episodes per system, 65 CVE clusters): DCv21 0.682 (2169 claims) vs DC 0.700 (1890 claims); difference DCv21 − DC = **-0.018** (95% paired CVE-clustered bootstrap CI [-0.047, +0.010]; CVE-clustered sign-flip p = 0.2269, two-sided, H0: no difference). Lower bound -0.047 ≤ −0.02.

Secondary: explanation–decision consistency DCv21 0.752 vs DC 0.960 (diff -0.208 [-0.263, -0.157], p = 0.0001); citation validity (claims citing call ids) DCv21 0.642 vs DC 0.334; hallucination DCv21 0.318 vs DC 0.300.

Reading: the primary difference is not significantly different from 0, but its CI extends below the −0.02 margin, so non-inferiority is not shown. The result depends on the evidence condition: with both systems judged on tool outputs only, DCv21 − DC = +0.073 [+0.043, +0.103] (DC's notes restate controller-derived facts that the raw outputs alone often do not show); with both judged with the controller `[state]`, -0.017 [-0.045, +0.010]. Per judge: gemma_4_31b +0.028 [-0.009, +0.062]; granite_41_30b -0.052 [-0.094, -0.010]; nemotron_super_49b -0.048 [-0.088, -0.011].

**Finding (validated-synthesis prompt).** The DCv21 synthesis prompt states the decision as "The decision is FIXED (do not change it): <status> …" (`agents/systems.py::_explain_validated`). Across all D7 test records, 40.5% of DCv21 notes whose decision is not `fixed` contain the word "FIXED" (DC: 0%), typically "… is marked as FIXED". The validator (`validate_note`) only checks that the decided status word occurs and that an `affected` note does not say "not affected", so these notes pass. Judges reading the note alone take "fixed" as the asserted status: for DCv21 notes with decision `under_investigation`, 28.4% of eligible-judge status readings are `fixed` (DC: 0.0%). This drives the consistency drop and the lower faithfulness of DCv21 status/conclusion claims (table below); fallback notes (which start "Status: <status>") are fully consistent.

DCv21 notes that are deterministic fallbacks: 6.2% of the sampled DCv21 episodes (all-records rate: 5.4%). Fallback notes are evaluated like any other note; results excluding them are below.

## Deviations / adaptations from PROTOCOL.md (1–5 fixed before any judge label was seen; 6 post hoc)

1. Data and systems: D7 test (`runs/d7/test/X2`), systems DC and DCv21, arms tracker/withheld/blind, 7 generators (granite41_30b added to the generator→family map: granite; judges of the generator's family are never used for system-level quantities). Sample: 168 cases (24 per generator), stratified by ecosystem × variant instead of variant only (D7 has four ecosystems).
2. Judges: only the three judges that PASSED E11 validation (gemma_4_31b, nemotron_super_49b with the `detailed thinking off` system prefix, granite_41_30b) are run; mistral_small_24b and qwen3_14b (failed validation) are not queried. Every validated judge labels every claim in main mode (agreement); cited / status / sensitivity modes are run for eligible judges only. Concurrency ≤ 16 per server.
3. Episode context block: the E11 template says "(Debian <release>)" and "source package"; for D7 it states the distro/release, ecosystem and advisory component, and, in the blind arm, that scanners were not available (`deepcti.judge.d7.context_block_d7`). Judge/extractor/status prompts and schemas are unchanged (their status definitions are worded for Debian packages; this applies equally to both systems).
4. Evidence: DC is judged with the replayed tool outputs + its controller `[state]` (E11 primary). DCv21's synthesis prompt never received the state (it received the raw cited outputs), so per the E11 rule (evidence = what the system's response LLM was given) its primary evidence is the tool outputs alone. Both symmetric alternatives are reported (neither with `[state]`; both with `[state]`).
5. H15 primary test pools all arms and generators (the pre-registration states no arm); per-arm and per-generator differences are secondary. The sign-flip p-value is a CVE-clustered label-swap test of the ratio difference (`deepcti.judge.paired.signflip_ratio_diff`). No-claim episodes contribute no claims (as E11).
6. Post hoc (added after the consistency result was seen; secondary, labelled as such): the "FIXED" word count in DCv21 notes (`note_checks_all_records.csv`) and the status-reading breakdown in the Finding paragraph; the status/conclusion vs factual-detail split (as E11 deviation 3); the judge-transfer perturbation check (its pool and controls are claims all three judges labelled supported, so it checks sensitivity to injected errors, not agreement with an independent reference).
